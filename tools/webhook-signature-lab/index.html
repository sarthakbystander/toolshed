Webhook Signature Lab

Work out exactly what a provider signs, generate the signature header, and check one you received — for GitHub, Stripe, Slack, Shopify, Standard Webhooks, or any plain HMAC. Your secret and payload never leave this tab.

1 · PROVIDER & INPUT

Read from this field only. It is never sent anywhere or saved.

Use the raw bytes, not a re-serialized object — whitespace and key order are part of the signature.
