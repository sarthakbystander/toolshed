Subtitle Studio

Open a SubRip (.srt) or WebVTT (.vtt) subtitle file and work on it like an editor: retime and stretch cues, repair overlaps, find and replace, rewrap long lines, check reading speed, split and merge, then export SRT, VTT, plain text or CSV. Everything is parsed and edited locally in your browser — no file ever leaves your machine.
